Our Process

We build the plan before we price the work.

Before we put a number on the job, we ask how the property operates, what deadline matters, and where the work could disrupt residents, tenants, or staff.

1

Understand the property.

Confirm budget, deadline, access, occupancy, and operating constraints.

2

Build the scope and schedule.

Define inclusions, phasing, preparation standards, and crew capacity.

3

Coordinate the work.

Agree on updates, access, changes, notifications, and site logistics.

4

Close out the job.

Review the work, address punch items, and deliver agreed documentation.
