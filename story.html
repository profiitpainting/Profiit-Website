Our Story

From local painting businesses to multi-market portfolios.

Field experience, local ownership, and operating systems built for property teams managing work across markets.

Daniel Diaz and Ryan Adamski learned the painting business in the field. Daniel started as a painting helper after moving from Venezuela to the United States and went on to lead operations and production across multiple states. Ryan grew up working in his family’s painting business and spent six years running his own company in Greater Philadelphia and Southern New Jersey.

Both went on to lead teams and build systems for larger painting organizations. They founded PROFiiT to give clients with buildings in several markets the same clarity, responsiveness, and accountability they would expect from a contractor they know locally.

Daniel F. Diaz

Co-Founder

Daniel grew up in Venezuela, began college at 15, and earned a business administration degree before working in oil-industry project management. At 23, he moved to the United States and began as a painting helper. He advanced through operations leadership at a multimillion-dollar general contractor, a national painting company, and a seven-state painting organization.

His process-over-price philosophy centers on clear scope, realistic planning, and accountability. A Painting Contractors Association member since 2022, Daniel has participated in conferences and speaking, supported the PCA Business Foundation, and contributed through the PCA en Español Advisory Committee.

Ryan Adamski

Co-Founder

Ryan grew up in his family’s painting business, beginning with removing switch plates and cleaning paintbrushes. Across nearly two decades in painting, he progressed through the trade and spent six years owning and operating a company in Greater Philadelphia and Southern New Jersey.

Ryan later helped large painting organizations build commercial estimating and production systems. He brings field knowledge, estimating discipline, and practical production planning together so scopes and schedules reflect how work will actually be delivered.
